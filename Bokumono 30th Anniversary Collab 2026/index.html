Bokumono 30th Anniversary Collab 2026

Sena's Contributions and Thoughts

Over half a year we've worked on this love letter to the beloved franchise Bokujo Monogatari, originally known as Harvest Moon in the west before changing to Story of Seasons in 2014.

With over 1000 unique pieces of artwork between 124 artists we have surely created something beautiful that I hope we all remember fondly for a long time. I am baffled, impressed, happy, and prideful over what we have accomplished, and am thankful for the friends made along the way.

Please see the full res version over on Dex's website.
